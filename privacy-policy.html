5. Use of AI-Powered Image Recognition

Identify Anything Lens Camera uses a third-party AI-powered image recognition service (powered by Google) to identify the subjects in the photos you scan and to provide diagnostic results. The service processes the image data you upload to generate identification and information results. Please be aware of the following details regarding the data shared with this service:

  • 1. Data Shared: When you use the scan or diagnose features, the photo you submit is sent to the AI-powered image recognition service to generate a response based on the image you provide. We do not collect or share any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email, or phone number, with this service.
  • 2. User Consent: Before your first scan, the App asks for your explicit consent to send your image to the AI service for analysis. By continuing, you consent to the processing of your image data by the service in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy. We ensure that the data is processed securely and in compliance with all applicable data protection laws.
  • 3. No Third-Party Use: We do not sell, rent, or lease your data to third parties. The image data shared with the AI service is used solely to provide the identification and diagnosis features of the App and is not used for any other purposes outside the scope of providing these services.

6. How to Opt Out
